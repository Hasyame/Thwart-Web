Thwart, the Marvel Champions companion

Your Marvel Champions collection, decks, campaigns and games, in English or French. Free in your browser, or installed on iPhone and iPad.

Where to

  • Cards: every card, searchable by name, text, trait, type, aspect or pack.
  • Rules: the rules reference, for the question that comes up mid-game.
  • Collection: tick the packs you own; the rest of the site follows.
  • Play: a campaign, custom game, a random game or a draft, with the clock and the encounter tracker.
  • History: every game you recorded, with its villain, heroes, rounds and time.
  • Statistics: win rates by hero, aspect, scenario and table size.
  • Decks: build them, sort them into folders, share them.
